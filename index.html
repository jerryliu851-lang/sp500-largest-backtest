Why these nine? ▸

Every strategy on this page except the index (and the index fund) was chosen knowing how it turned out. Apple was the best mega-cap of 2020 — unknowable then. The 'Magnificent 7' earned that name because those seven won. Picking 'the top fund since 2020' picks it because it outperformed. The controls below exist to strip out that hindsight.

  • An active fund chosen by a rule applied as of Jan 2020 — largest actively-managed US equity fund by total net assets = AGTHX (American Funds Growth Fund of America, ~$179B; net ER 0.59%) — not the best performer.
  • The median large-cap active fund, via SPIVA (see the explainer below) — the honest population answer, not one cherry-picked winner.
  • MSFT control = the largest company in 2020 that was not Apple (same rule, different draw).
Index vs. active, explained ▸

An S&P 500 index mutual fund (VFIAX) holds the same 500 companies as the SPY ETF — the only real difference is the expense ratio (VFIAX 0.04% vs SPY 0.0945%), which is why VFIAX edged SPY by a hair. That is not a new strategy. The genuinely different case is an actively managed fund (AGTHX), where a manager picks the holdings — and here it lagged the index net of its 0.59% fee, paying $10,319 in fees (15× VFIAX's $692) to do so. Per S&P's SPIVA U.S. Scorecard (Year-End 2024), 76.26% of large-cap active funds underperformed the S&P 500 over the five years. AGTHX is one fund; SPIVA is the honest median answer. Cherry-picking a known winner (Fidelity Contrafund, +201.5%) would flatter active management — which is exactly the selection error this page is about.

ASSUMPTIONS & METHODOLOGY

Data: Yahoo Finance daily closes. Returns are TOTAL RETURN (dividends reinvested, split-adjusted). A price-return toggle is provided.

Funds: Fund values are NAV net of expense ratio — the fee is already embedded in the price series. Each fund row's "fees paid" makes that embedded cost explicit: final × ((1+ER)^years − 1). Mutual funds' annual capital-gains distribution taxes are NOT modeled (a simplification that would make active funds look slightly worse than shown here). Dividend tax is not separately modeled for any strategy.

Markets were closed 2026-08-08/09 (weekend); the final valuation uses the last close, Fri 2026-08-07.

Tax assumptions: Assumes a single federal filer. Long-term (>1yr) 15%, short-term 24% (adjustable). State taxes ignored. Capital-loss offsetting across strategies/segments is not modeled. Mag 7 annual-rebalance gains are treated long-term (~368-day holds between rebalances); the terminal sale is treated long-term on an average-cost basis — a stated simplification.

No fabrication: every fund ticker, expense ratio, and constituent on this page is verified against a cited source (see below).

REBALANCE TAX LOG

Static equal-weight is rebalanced to exactly 1/7 on the first trading day of each year. Overweight positions are sold (gain realized vs. average cost basis, taxed long-term), and the post-tax cash buys underweight positions pro-rata to their shortfall. The terminal sale at the end of the period dominates the total tax bill.
